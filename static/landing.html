How to Play KATCHO

A high-stakes party battle of memory retention, social deduction, and cryptographic truth verification.

PHASE 1
edit_note

Secret Word Lock-In

Every player secretly writes and locks their custom words.

  • 1 or 2 Words Mode: For rooms under 7 players, host can enable 2 words per player for deeper mystery.
  • Encrypted & Private: Secret words are never visible to opponents during entry or lobby.
  • Ready Check: Host starts the reveal sequence once everyone locks in their words.
PHASE 2
visibility

The Visual Moderator

Words flash rapidly across the central stage in a randomized sequence.

  • 2 Full Loops: The sequence flashes twice at 3 seconds per word. Keep your eyes glued!
  • Anti-Cheat Lock: Private scratchpads are automatically locked during the flash so nobody can take live notes.
  • Memory & Association: Memorize the vocabulary and link words to players' personalities.
PHASE 3
psychology

Accusations & Truth-Lock

Players take turns deducing who submitted which secret word.

  • Make an Accusation: Pick any active player and enter the word you believe is theirs.
  • Truth-Locked Verification: The accused receives an immutable server check: YES (Correct) or NO (Wrong). Zero lying is possible!
  • Turn Outcome: Correct guess keeps your turn! Wrong guess passes your turn to the accused.
PHASE 4
emoji_events

Victory & The Unmasking

The last surviving uncaught player wins the crown and match honors.

  • Full Reveal Table: Game over reveals every player's hidden words, accuracy rates, and elimination chain.
  • Unanimous Draw Vote: In close stalemates, the turn player can call a draw vote requiring 100% agreement.
  • Play Again: Reset the room in 1 tap with the same players for the next round!
lightbulb

Pro Deduction Strategy

Watch eye movements during Phase 2. Players often react subtly when their own secret word flashes! Use turn passes strategically to force opponents into making risky guesses.

KATCHO Official Rules & Strategy Guide

🎮 Object of the Game: Memorize the flashing words presented by the Visual Moderator, deduce which opponent wrote which secret word, and catch all opponents before you are unmasked!
1

Phase 1: Word Entry & Mode Selection

Each player privately submits their secret words into the encrypted terminal. The host chooses between 1 Word Mode (fast, intense) or 2 Words Mode (double mystery for 2–6 players). No other player can see what you write.

2

Phase 2: Visual Moderator Flash (Anti-Cheat Locked)

All secret words are shuffled and flashed sequentially on the central stage (3 seconds per word). The sequence repeats twice. During this phase, all scratchpads are automatically locked to prevent real-time typing. Sharp memory is your only weapon!

3

Phase 3: Turn Guessing & Truth-Lock

Active player chooses an opponent and calls out their guessed word. The accused sees an unhackable prompt: YES (That's my word) or NO (Not my word). The system checks against the encrypted backend so players cannot cheat or bluff their own word!

✅ If Correct: Accused is eliminated (or loses that word). Guesser keeps their turn!
❌ If Wrong: Turn passes immediately to the accused player.

4

Phase 4: Match Victory & Reveal Table

The last surviving uncaught player wins! A complete end-game reveal table unmasks every player's submitted words, deduction accuracy scores, and timestamps.

⭐

Special Actions: Turn Pass & Draw Vote

Pass Turn: If you are unsure of any word, pass your turn randomly to avoid handing an opponent momentum.
Call Draw: In prolonged deadlocks, trigger an unanimous vote where every player must agree to end in a gentleman's draw.
