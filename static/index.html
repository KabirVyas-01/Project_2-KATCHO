How to Play KATCHO

1
Phase 1: Secret Word Entry (1 or 2 Words)

Every player secretly enters their words. If under 7 players, host can choose 1 or 2 words per player. Once locked in, host starts reveal!

2
Phase 2: The Visual Moderator

All submitted words flash one by one on the Top Stage. The sequence plays once, then repeats a second time. Memorize what you see!

3
Phase 3: Turn Guessing & Truth Lock

When it is your turn, pick a player and guess their secret word. The accused sees a Truth-Locked button (YES if right, NO if wrong). No lying possible!

4
Turn Outcomes

YES (Correct): Accused is Caught (or loses that word). Guesser keeps their turn!
NO (Wrong): Turn passes immediately to the accused player.

5
Phase 4: Victory

The last remaining uncaught player wins the game! A full reveal table unmasks every player's secret words, accuracy, and elimination history.
